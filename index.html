Overview

ABVSoft (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy describes information that may be processed by the app and by third-party services used to support advertising and app functionality.

1. Information We Collect

The app does not require you to create an account. We do not ask you to provide your name, phone number, contacts, or precise GPS location to play the quiz.

The app or its third-party SDKs may process technical and usage information, such as app interactions, diagnostics, device or advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, and advertising-related data, depending on your device settings, consent choices, and the services available in your region.

2. Advertising and Consent

The app may use Yandex Mobile Ads and related mediation or advertising services to display ads. These providers may collect or process data for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, personalization.

Where required, the app provides a consent choice for personalized advertising. You can also review available advertising and privacy controls in the app and in your device settings. The options and legal requirements may vary by region.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To operate and maintain the app and its features.
  • To remember local app settings and game progress where supported.
  • To display, measure, and improve advertising, subject to applicable consent and settings.
  • To diagnose errors, protect the service, and prevent misuse.

4. Local Storage

App settings and gameplay data may be stored locally on your device. We do not operate an ABVSoft account system for this app. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored data; backed-up or platform-managed data may be handled separately by your device provider.

5. Third-Party Services

Third-party SDKs and services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Their processing is governed by those policies, not solely by this document. Please review the relevant provider policies, including the policies of Yandex services used in the app.

Yandex privacy information: Yandex Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when needed to provide app features, deliver or measure advertising, maintain security, or comply with applicable law.

7. Data Retention and Security

Data stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted or the app is uninstalled, subject to device and backup behavior. Third-party providers determine retention for data they process under their own policies. We take reasonable steps in the app’s design to limit unnecessary collection, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising providers and the app’s configuration may apply additional restrictions depending on age, region, and applicable law. If you believe personal information has been provided to us inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter.

9. Your Choices

You can manage available consent choices in the app, change your device’s advertising settings, limit ad personalization where supported, or stop using and uninstall the app. Some settings are controlled by third-party platforms and may differ by device or region.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page with an updated date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact ABVSoft at abvsoft.apps@gmail.com.
